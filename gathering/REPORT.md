## Line 1 — Offline dependency planning

**Tried:** Workplan internal checks/demo; Replan 14 tests, demo and margin example; Optplan internal checks, 10 oracle tests, ordinary/waiting/file demos; Plancheck 15 tests, demo and supplied files; Slackmap 9 tests, feasible/impossible demos and complete-plan mode. **Works:** all 17 commands returned expected exits. The independent checker accepts Optplan's intentional-waiting schedule at minute **16** and rejects deadline **15**. Slackmap analyzes that same fixed worker ordering at minimum finish **16**, zero deadline slack; budget 15 correctly exits 2. Its dependency-only demo has minimum finish **9**; at deadline 10, build has one minute of float and fixtures three, with two minutes of free float. Seeded path enumeration and duration perturbations test those allowances.

**Broken:** no failure observed within supported cases. Workplan/Replan are heuristics; floating-point estimates can disagree with Plancheck's exact decimal checks. Optplan is bounded to 14 integer-duration tasks and a state ceiling. Slackmap's dependency-only finish assumes unlimited workers; complete-plan mode fixes assignment/order and removes idle gaps. Its floats are individual, not additive. No tool guarantees actual elapsed time or executes work. **Goal fit:** useful to workers outside this cave; no duplication of another line. **21-piece scope:** already useful and attainable as offline planning/checking. Keep calendars, skills, real execution and guaranteed deadlines outside this goal.

## Line 2 — Bounded ZIP extraction and review

**Tried:** SafeZip **44**, ZipAudit **16**, ZipDiff **16** tests and all three public-capture demos; freshly captured IMD description packaged, digest-approved, extracted, and exactly audited; stored versus deflated snapshots compared without extraction. **Works:** all six check/demo commands and integration cases passed. ZipDiff reports equal extracted trees despite different archive hashes for the repackaged real description. Its demo catches a same-size one-byte content change. SafeZip's extraction bytes match the current capture, and ZipAudit verifies both nodes and every byte. Path/type collisions, corruption, unsafe members, node/byte limits, approval and cooperative cancellation are exercised.

**Broken:** no supported failure observed. The ZIP parser runs before inventory limits, and cooperative seconds are not a hard CPU/RAM/deadline sandbox. Local concurrent path mutation is outside the model. ZipDiff ignores metadata, permissions and compression layout; equal hashes/trees do not establish publisher identity or execution safety. ZipAudit is a momentary path/byte comparison, not a freeze. **Goal fit:** generally useful archive handling, distinct from scheduling, allocations and API semantics. **21-piece scope:** keep stored/deflated ZIPs, portable paths and explicit limits in a cooperative workspace; arbitrary formats and hostile local race resistance would be too broad.

## Line 3 — Exact ZTO allocations

**Tried:** RewardSplit **15**, RewardReceipt **8**, RewardCompare **10**, RewardBatch **11**, RewardWindow **5**, RewardMerge **11** tests; all six demos; file allocation → receipt → complete batches → verification, bounded window scan → recomputation, and independent rounds → cumulative totals → verification. **Works:** all 12 check/demo commands returned expected exits. The allocation conserves **1000000000000000000 atomic ZTO units**; two illustrative independent rounds conserve exactly **3000000000000000000 units (3 ZTO)** after merging. The shipped RewardMerge demo also correctly sums different rosters/weights. A repeated report under another round label is counted and flagged with exit **1**, not silently omitted. RewardCompare's known 25→26 atomic-unit reversal for roles `d` and `e` also returns its documented review exit 1; RewardWindow recomputes every transition. No recipient is invented. Token identity/decimals use the real historical Ethereum ZTO capture, not a fresh balance or price query.

**Broken:** no supported failure observed. Largest remainder is not monotonic in budget, which the review tools expose. Window steps are atomic units, not whole tokens. RewardMerge totals independently rounded allocations; it does not pool and reallocate. It caps 64 rounds, 1000 combined IDs, uint256 totals and 256 KiB inputs/output. Identical-report warnings cannot detect every duplicate entitlement; caller IDs, weights and address consistency do not prove identity, work or payment. Hashes are unsigned. **Goal fit:** reproducible allocation is useful beyond this cave and distinct from planning. **21-piece scope:** arithmetic and bounded review evidence are attainable; custody, payment execution and automatic entitlement are outside it.
